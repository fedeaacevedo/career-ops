## Best Immediate Targets

### 1. VTEX - Engineering Manager, Cloud Storage

- **Why it fits:** Manage infrastructure and software engineers; own storage backbone; platform engineering; developer velocity; self-service tooling; high availability; automation.
- **Profile match:** CTO/Technology Manager + AWS/infrastructure + observability + team leadership maps well.
- **Risks:** Fluent English; 4+ years management requirement; deep storage/database ecosystem expected.
- **Recommendation:** Evaluate first.

### 2. Bitso - Senior Engineering Manager

- **Why it may fit:** LATAM fintech + senior engineering management is directly aligned with the leadership track.
- **Profile match:** likely strong if the scope is infrastructure, platform, security, cloud, operations, or engineering delivery.
- **Risks:** Need JD verification; could be product engineering or domain-specific.
- **Recommendation:** Evaluate second after fetching full JD.

### 3. GitLab - Staff Technical Program Manager

- **Why it fits:** Cross-functional engineering programs, ambiguity, technical planning, CI/CD, cloud infrastructure, APIs, distributed systems, senior stakeholders.
- **Profile match:** Mercedes-Benz PM + CTO + vendor/stakeholder/program work is relevant.
- **Risks:** Staff-level TPM bar, exceptional async English, remote location currently listed for Europe.
- **Recommendation:** Evaluate if location eligibility allows Argentina or global remote.
