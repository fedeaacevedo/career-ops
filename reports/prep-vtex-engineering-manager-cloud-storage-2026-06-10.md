## Main Match Points

- **Technical leadership:** The role manages a mixed team of software and infrastructure engineers, which maps well to Federico's CTO / Technology Manager / DevOps Lead profile.
- **Platform and infrastructure:** VTEX wants a platform-minded leader for SQL, NoSQL, search, object storage, integration libraries, SDKs, and self-service tooling.
- **Operational maturity:** The role focuses on availability, performance, resilience, automation, and replacing manual requests with self-service systems.
- **Developer velocity:** Federico can frame Seeker and Estudiantes as examples of improving delivery, support, reliability, and operational processes across teams.
- **LATAM context:** VTEX is a LATAM company with Spanish/Portuguese regional context, which is better than English-only US/EU roles.

## Main Weaknesses

- **Fluent English:** VTEX explicitly asks whether the candidate is comfortable conducting meetings in English with international stakeholders.
- **4+ years management screen:** The application asks for at least 4 years as a manager. Federico has leadership experience, but the formal timeline may need careful framing.
- **Deep storage scale:** The JD asks for massive-scale experience across Aurora/Postgres, SQL Server, Redis, DynamoDB, OpenSearch/Solr, and object storage. Federico has relevant AWS/database exposure, but not obviously massive-scale storage platform ownership.
- **Engineering Manager title:** Federico has CTO / Technology Manager / DevOps Lead titles, but not a clean long-running Engineering Manager title.
- **Internal developer platform:** Experience with libraries, SDKs, golden paths, and storage-as-a-service needs to be translated from platform/process work rather than claimed too strongly.

### 30/60/90-Day Plan

**First 30 days**
- Map the storage ecosystem, team topology, incident history, operational bottlenecks, and top developer pain points.
- Meet product squads consuming storage services and identify recurring manual requests.
- Review reliability metrics, on-call load, backlog, SLAs/SLOs, and current self-service capabilities.

**Days 31-60**
- Prioritize one or two high-impact golden paths: provisioning, access, monitoring, backups, or performance visibility.
- Define a roadmap balancing reliability, developer velocity, and operational risk.
- Establish lightweight metrics: request lead time, incident volume, MTTR, availability, toil, and adoption of self-service flows.

**Days 61-90**
- Ship the first measurable self-service improvement.
- Reduce at least one recurring manual workflow.
- Present a storage platform roadmap with technical debt, automation, reliability, and developer enablement priorities.
